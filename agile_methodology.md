# Agile Methodology

## What is Agile?
Agile is a software development methodology where software is developed and delivered in small steps.

## Agile Principles

1. Customer Satisfaction
2. Continuous Improvement
3. Team Collaboration
4. Fast Delivery
5. Adaptability

## Scrum Roles

1. Product Owner
2. Scrum Master
3. Development Team

## Scrum Events

1. Sprint Planning
2. Daily Stand-up Meeting
3. Sprint Review
4. Sprint Retrospective

## What is Sprint?
A Sprint is a fixed period in which a team completes planned work.

## What is Daily Stand-up?
A short daily meeting where team members discuss:
- What did I do yesterday?
- What will I do today?
- Are there any blockers?
